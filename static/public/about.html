About PARAKH AI

Empowering transparent and efficient public procurement.

Our Mission

In public procurement on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), evaluating tender bids across technical qualifications, statutory financial thresholds, MSME exemptions, and quality certifications is an intensely manual process.

PARAKH AI is designed to slash review time by combining Tender Intelligence, Requirement-Evidence Mapping, and Multi-Source Verification, ensuring officers have clear, verified data to make confident decisions.
